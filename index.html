Who we are

A trading partner, not a middleman.

A-One International General Trading connects manufacturers with buyers who need reliable supply — not just a quotation.

Most sourcing problems are not price problems. They are communication problems: a spec that shifted, an inspection nobody did, a document that arrived after the vessel. A-One exists to close those gaps. One point of contact owns your order from enquiry through delivered cargo.

  • Suppliers vetted before we place an order
  • Goods inspected against the agreed specification
  • Export documentation, customs and freight arranged in-house
  • Part-loads consolidated so you ship full rather than often

What we stand for

Five principles we do not trade away.

01

Say the real number

Our quotation is what the goods cost delivered, including the parts people leave out. If a price looks too good, we explain why ours is not.

02

Check before you ship

Inspection is not a formality. Goods are examined against the agreed specification before despatch, and you see the report.

03

Own the problem

When something goes wrong — and in trade it eventually does — we tell you early, with options, rather than late with excuses.

04

Protect the relationship

We would rather decline an order we cannot execute properly than take it and disappoint a buyer we intend to keep.

05

Keep the paperwork clean

Correct documentation is not administration, it is cash flow. Errors on a certificate of origin are measured in demurrage.

What we trade

Six divisions, one supply chain.

Each division is run by buyers who know their category — the mills, the grades, the seasonality and the certifications that matter.

Foodstuff & FMCG

Staples and fast-moving consumer goods in bulk and retail packs, with the shelf-life and labelling your market requires.

  • Rice, wheat flour, sugar
  • Pulses, lentils, beans
  • Edible oils & canned goods
  • Tea, coffee, beverages
  • Private-label packing

Building Materials

Project-quantity supply for contractors, scheduled against your construction programme rather than dumped on site at once.

  • Cement, steel & aggregates
  • Tiles, marble & granite
  • Sanitaryware & fittings
  • Plumbing & electrical
  • Doors, windows & hardware

Electronics

Consumer and commercial electronics supplied with the voltage, plug type and compliance marking your destination market requires.

  • Home & kitchen appliances
  • Air conditioning & fans
  • Mobile & IT accessories
  • LED lighting & fixtures
  • Batteries & power supplies

Textiles & Apparel

Fabric, made-ups and finished garments produced to your tech pack, with a sample approved before bulk runs.

  • Cotton & blended fabric
  • Uniforms & workwear

  • Curtains & upholstery
  • Bags & accessories

Industrial Supplies

Maintenance, repair and operations consumables kept moving against your schedule, so a shutdown is never waiting on a fastener.

  • PPE & safety equipment
  • Hand & power tools

Seasonal produce and agricultural commodities, contracted against written quality parameters rather than a handshake.

  • Grains & cereals

  • Animal feed & additives
  • Sugar & sweeteners

Not on the list? These divisions are where we already hold relationships — they are a starting point, not a boundary. Send the specification and we will tell you plainly whether we can source it properly.

What we do

Everything between your order and your yard.

Take the whole chain or take one link of it. Most buyers start with sourcing and hand us more once they see the paperwork come back clean.

01

Sourcing & procurement

We identify suppliers, verify them and negotiate on your behalf, then manage the order through production.

02

Quality inspection

Goods checked against the agreed specification before they leave the supplier, with a photographic report to you.

03

Export documentation

Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and the bank documents a letter of credit demands.

04

Freight forwarding

Sea, air and road freight booked against your delivery window, with the cargo tracked until it lands.

05

Customs clearance

Declarations prepared and lodged correctly, because a classification error is a cost that compounds daily.

06

Warehousing & consolidation

Part-loads from several suppliers combined into one container, plus storage, re-packing and labelling.

How it works

Four steps from enquiry to delivery.

01

Enquiry

02

Sourcing & quote

Inspection
